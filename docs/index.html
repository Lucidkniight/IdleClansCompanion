Features

Everything you need, in one place

Built for Idle Clans players who want more than the game client alone can offer.

🎮

Multi-Account Manager

Detect all open Idle Clans clients instantly. Switch between accounts with one click and see live window previews updating every second.

✨

XP / GP Calculator

Calculate XP per hour and time to goal level for any task. Factor in tools, gear, upgrades, and mastery capes. Includes a full profit-per-hour breakdown with live market prices.

🏪

Live Market Prices

Browse real-time buy and sell prices for any item. View order books, 24h price history charts, and price averages over 1, 7, and 30 days.

⚔️

Combat Calculator

Estimate DPS, hit chance, and time-to-kill for any monster or boss. Configure your gear and combat style, and see expected loot value per hour at market price.

🏆

Clan Cup Tracker

Live leaderboard filterable by skill, boss kills, speed runs, and total points. Search any clan to see all their standings at once.

🔎

Player Lookup

Search any player by name. See every skill level and XP, upgrades, enchantments, PvM kill counts, game mode, and last active time.

✅

Completionist Tracker

Track your progress toward the completionist cape. See exactly which requirements remain and get a time estimate for each one.

🐉

Bestiary

Browse every monster and boss in the game. See drop tables, combat stats, weaknesses, respawn timers, and filter by combat type.

🛡️

Clan Overview

View a full XP leaderboard for any clan. Filter by skill, toggle lifetime vs contributed XP, and jump directly to any member's profile.

📖

Wiki Search

Search the Idle Clans wiki without leaving the app. Results open instantly in your default browser so you never lose your place.

📝

Notepad

A persistent notepad saved locally. Keep goals, clan notes, to-do lists, or anything else you want close at hand while you play.

🚀

Client Launcher

Launch multiple Idle Clans game instances at once directly from the companion. Point it at your game executable and spin up as many clients as you need.

Ready to play smarter?

Free to download, no account required. Installs in seconds and updates automatically when new versions are released.
