Idle Clans Player Lookup — Search Any Player's Stats

Search any player's skills, upgrades, and profile in seconds

Search any Idle Clans player by username and see their full profile: every skill level and XP total, upgrades, enchantment boosts, equipped gear, PvM kill counts, game mode, and last active time — all in one place, without visiting their in-game profile.

↓  Download for Windows ▶  Try it in your browser

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning — click "More info" → "Run anyway". The app is open source and safe.

What it does

  • Every skill level and XP total for any player
  • Upgrades, enchantment boosts, and equipped gear
  • PvM kill counts and completionist progress
  • Game mode and last-active time
  • Recent search history saved locally

Part of Idle Clans Companion

Player Lookup is one tool inside Idle Clans Companion, a free Windows desktop app with a full multi-account manager, XP/profit calculators, live market prices, combat calculator, and more.
